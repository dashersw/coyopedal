# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

In short: be decent to each other. Assume the person on the other end is trying
to help, say what you mean about the code rather than about them, and take
correction the way you would want to give it. Harassment, insults and personal
attacks are not welcome here, and neither is making someone's identity the
subject.

Maintainers are responsible for what stays in issues, pull requests and
discussions, and may edit or remove anything that does not belong.

Report a problem to **armagan@coyotiv.com**. Reports are handled privately, and
whoever reports one is not the subject of the conversation that follows.
