## How to sign

Open a pull request. A bot will comment asking you to sign, and you reply on the
pull request with exactly:

```text
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

Your signature — your GitHub username, the date and the commit — is recorded in
[`signatures/cla.json`](signatures/cla.json) in this repository. You sign once,
and it covers everything you contribute afterwards.
